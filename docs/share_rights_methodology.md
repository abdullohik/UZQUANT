# Share-rights methodology

Security rights are security-class-specific and point-in-time. The schema supports voting, minimum/formula/cumulative dividend, liquidation preference, conversion, redemption, participation, pre-emptive rights and nominal value with an exact source provision.

Phase 5 did not locate authoritative charter or issue-prospectus provisions for the test securities. Openinfo dividend disclosures prove observed class-specific dividend treatment, but do not prove permanent economics. Accordingly, AGMK, AGMKP, UZTL and UZTLP receive summaries of the observed dividend treatment with `LOW` rights confidence. All governing right fields remain null. Preferred economics are never inferred from a `P` suffix.

This deliberately permits downstream analysis to return `VALUATION INSUFFICIENT` when unknown rights are material. A later authoritative charter may append a higher-confidence rights version; it must not overwrite earlier evidence.
