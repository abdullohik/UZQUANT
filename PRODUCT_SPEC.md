# UZQUANT — Product Specification

## Product Overview

UZQUANT is a systematic equity-research and investment-intelligence platform for Uzbekistan's public equity market, initially focused on securities listed on the Republican Stock Exchange "Toshkent."

The purpose of UZQUANT is to analyze publicly available financial, market, ownership, corporate, and macroeconomic information more comprehensively and systematically than a human investor could reasonably do manually.

The platform should help identify potentially mispriced securities while accounting for the characteristics of the Uzbek market, particularly low liquidity, concentrated ownership, stale prices, limited free float, different share classes, fragmented disclosures, and inconsistent data quality.

UZQUANT is not intended to be a simple stock screener.

It should eventually function as an institutional-style research and decision-support system.

---

## Core Objective

UZQUANT should ultimately answer:

> Which Uzbek-listed securities appear attractive, why are they attractive, what are they approximately worth, what could cause the valuation gap to close, what risks exist, how liquid are they, how much can realistically be invested, and how confident should we be in the conclusion?

The system should distinguish between three separate questions:

1. Is this a good company?
2. Is this specific security attractively priced?
3. Can an investor realistically capture the opportunity given liquidity and market structure?

A good company does not automatically mean a good investment.

A cheap security does not automatically mean an investable opportunity.

## Market Philosophy

UZQUANT is being designed specifically for a frontier-market environment.

The Uzbek market may contain:

- securities with very low trading activity
- stale quoted prices
- tiny free floats
- highly concentrated ownership
- government or strategic controlling shareholders
- wide bid/ask spreads
- large percentage price movements caused by very few trades
- inconsistent financial disclosure quality
- common and preferred shares with different economics
- corporate actions that distort historical prices
- securities that appear profitable on paper but cannot realistically be entered or exited at scale

UZQUANT must therefore treat liquidity, data quality, ownership, security rights, and execution capacity as core parts of investment analysis.

---

## Competitive Advantage

UZQUANT should not attempt to compete with global firms through high-frequency trading or faster execution.

Its potential advantage should come from:

> Collecting, organizing, verifying, and analyzing Uzbekistan's fragmented public financial information better than the typical market participant.

The system should attempt to find opportunities through better information processing, better valuation, better risk assessment, and better understanding of market liquidity.

## Realizable Returns

One of the defining principles of UZQUANT is the distinction between:

THEORETICAL RETURN

and

REALIZABLE RETURN.

A security may display a 300%, 500%, or even larger historical gain while having traded only a very small number of shares.

UZQUANT must therefore consider whether an investor could realistically have entered and exited a position at the displayed prices.

Liquidity-adjusted investment performance is more important than purely theoretical portfolio performance.

---

## Explainability

UZQUANT should behave like a skeptical institutional analyst.

It should be able to explain:

- why a security appears cheap or expensive
- what assumptions drive its valuation
- why the market may be mispricing it
- what catalysts might close the valuation gap
- what risks may explain the low valuation
- whether the price is reliable
- whether the security is sufficiently liquid
- whether the investor could realistically exit
- what data is uncertain
- what would change the investment conclusion

The system should never produce unexplained BUY signals.

---

## Confidence

Investment attractiveness and confidence must be treated separately.

A security may appear extremely attractive while the available evidence is weak.

For example:

Investment Attractiveness: High

Confidence: Low

This should result in a cautious conclusion rather than an aggressive recommendation.

---

## Initial Scope

The initial version of UZQUANT is a:

RESEARCH AND DECISION-SUPPORT PLATFORM.

It is not initially intended to automatically execute trades.

Live brokerage execution, if ever added, should only be considered after the research system, historical validation, liquidity models, and paper-trading system have been extensively tested.

---

## Product Principles

UZQUANT should always prioritize:

Data Integrity > Model Complexity

Realizable Returns > Paper Returns

Explainability > Black-Box Predictions

Risk Management > Maximum Theoretical Return

Primary Evidence > Convenient Estimates

Point-in-Time Accuracy > Historical Convenience

Security-Level Analysis > Company-Level Assumptions

When reliable analysis cannot be performed, UZQUANT should prefer:

DATA INSUFFICIENT

rather than generating a misleading investment recommendation.

---

## Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective is to create a systematic Uzbekistan-focused investment-intelligence platform capable of continuously analyzing the investable public-equity universe.

It should function as a combination of:

- financial-data platform
- equity research system
- quantitative research engine
- valuation platform
- liquidity-risk system
- event-monitoring platform
- portfolio research tool

The long-term competitive advantage should not be:

> Trade faster than everyone else.

It should be:

> Understand the Uzbek public-equity market more systematically, comprehensively, and accurately than the typical market participant.
