# Peer Multiple Evidence

Assessment date: 2026-09-27

No peer-derived valuation multiple was approved.

The minimum defensible peer count is three comparable issuers with verified identity, sector/business comparability, compatible reporting perimeter and period, normalized units/currency, verified share counts, and sufficiently current observed prices. The supported universe does not meet that condition:

- IPKY is the only supported bank.
- KASUP is the only supported insurer.
- AGMKP is the only supported mining issuer.
- UZTL and QZSM are both routed through the general engine but telecom and cement are not economic peers.
- AGMK/AGMKP and KASU/KASUP are share classes of the same issuers and cannot be counted as independent companies.

Current P/B or P/E components can be computed as diagnostics for several securities, but unknown reporting perimeter—and interim-period earnings in some cases—prevents treating those ratios as compatible peer evidence. No market ratio was converted into a fair value.
