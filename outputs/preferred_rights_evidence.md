# Preferred-Rights Evidence Review

Scope was deliberately limited after IPKY achieved the sprint's first real
valuation.

## AGMKP

The official AGMK issuer dividend page and dividend-policy document report a
preferred dividend equal to 25% of nominal value; the issuer page reports
978.5 UZS per preferred share against 3,914 UZS nominal value. The exchange
identifies AGMKP as preferred ISIN `UZ700055K015`.

This is meaningful authoritative evidence, but it is not yet enough to mark the
entire current rights package `HIGH`: current effective charter provisions,
cumulation, liquidation preference, voting triggers, conversion/redemption,
and temporal applicability have not all been extracted and versioned. The
production blocker `PREFERRED_SECURITY_RIGHTS_UNVERIFIED` therefore remains.

Sources:

- `https://agmk.uz/index.php/ru/menu/viplata-dividendov`
- `https://agmk.uz/uploads/docs/666bd4cb2106bcd19f71525ce5f455c1.pdf`
- `https://uzse.uz/isu_infos/UZ700055K015/detail`

## KASUP

The exchange identifies KASUP as preferred ISIN `UZ702866K013` and publishes a
2025 charter attachment. Openinfo also publishes class-specific dividend
disclosures, including a 2025 supervisory-board recommendation showing 6.25%
of nominal for preferred shares. These observations do not establish the full
contractual rights package or whether the recommendation became an effective
dividend obligation.

No share-right field was inferred from ticker suffix or trading behavior. The
production blocker `PREFERRED_SECURITY_RIGHTS_UNVERIFIED` remains.

Sources:

- `https://uzse.uz/isu_infos/UZ702866K013/detail`
- `https://new.openinfo.uz/en/facts/41/445`
